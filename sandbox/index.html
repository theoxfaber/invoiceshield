InvoiceShield sandbox: try to get a payment out of the gate

Upload an invoice PDF and an AI accounts-payable agent will try to pay exactly what it asks. A deterministic Rust gate decides PAY, REVIEW or BLOCK. The payee can only ever be the vendor's account in the vendor master, so the number to watch is paid to a wrong account.

Synthetic data only: every vendor, GSTIN, PO and account here is fictional. Nothing is ever paid (there is no payment rail). This runs on a free hosting tier that spins down when idle, so the first request after a quiet spell can take up to a minute. Limits: a few attempts per minute and a daily cap per visitor, a global daily cap, and a daily model-call budget; past that budget the model is not called and every invoice goes to REVIEW. Do not upload real invoices or personal data.
